# composer-mcp

This repo is public. Material that must never be published goes in `_internal-only/`
(gitignored; confirm with `git check-ignore` before writing there).

When a Logi Composer, Dundas or Simba Intelligence fact is missing, the Atlassian connector
(wiki and issue tracker) may be read, never written. Mirror what you learn into
`_internal-only/` or a private repo, never into a tracked file here, and never back into
Atlassian.
